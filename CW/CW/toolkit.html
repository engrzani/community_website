Community Toolkit

Resources, guides and tips to help you make the most of your skill-sharing journey

Getting Started Guide

New to skill sharing? Read our step-by-step guide to setting up your profile, finding matches and scheduling your first session. It is easier than you think!

Safety Tips

Your safety matters. Always meet in public spaces for your first session, let someone know where you are going, and trust your instincts. Review our full safety checklist before your first swap.

Teaching Best Practices

Sharing a skill is an art in itself. Break complex topics into small steps, be patient, and encourage questions. Our tips will help you become a confident community tutor.

Learning Effectively

Come prepared with questions, take notes, and practice between sessions. Consistent effort, even 15 minutes a day, makes all the difference when picking up a new skill.

Hosting a Workshop

Want to teach a group? We can help you organise a community workshop. Find a venue, set an agenda, and invite participants through our platform.

Upcoming Events

Stay informed about community meet-ups, seasonal skill fairs, and special workshops. Check back regularly or sign up for our newsletter to never miss an event.

Ready to Lend a Hand?

Join our growing community of neighbours who teach, learn and help each other every day.
